# Herdr artwork

`herdr-logo.svg` is the unmodified Herdr logo from [herdrdev/herdr](https://github.com/herdrdev/herdr/blob/main/assets/logo.svg), distributed under the Apache License 2.0. A copy of that license is included in `HERDR-LICENSE.txt`.

The addon-specific `icon.tga`, `minimap.tga` and `portrait.tga` are modified, resized TGA conversions of that logo at 64×64, 32×32 and 128×128 pixels, respectively. The image content is unchanged; the dimensions and file format were changed for WoW's texture requirements. They replace the original T3 Code artwork.

This is an unofficial WoW integration. Herdr names and marks identify the integrated software; no sponsorship or endorsement is claimed. The addon and bridge's original MIT license remains in the repository's `LICENSE` file.
